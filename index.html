Customer health dashboard

Sample data for 10 fictional SaaS accounts. Tap an account to see why it scored that way and get a ready-to-send email.

How scoring works: seat usage 40%, NPS 25%, open support tickets 20%, days since last login 15%. Healthy is 70 or above, Watch is 45 to 69, At risk is below 45. Within each group, accounts are listed by estimated loss, largest first. Estimated loss is annual revenue × (100 minus the score)%, a rough guide and not a forecast. Suggested discounts assume a 25% profit margin: riskier accounts get a larger offer, never above 10%. A discount always trims the margin on that account, so it is worth offering only if it keeps enough accounts. All data and revenue figures are fictional.
